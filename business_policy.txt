Enterprise Customer Retention Policy

Customer retention is a strategic priority for the organization.

Customers with declining revenue, low transaction frequency,
or declining profitability should be reviewed by the account
management team.

High-risk customers should receive a structured account review.

The account management team should evaluate pricing,
customer engagement, product adoption, service quality,
and competitive pressure.

Customers with strong revenue and high profitability should
be considered for cross-selling and expansion opportunities.

Business decisions should be supported by measurable evidence
from enterprise analytics.

Customer accounts identified as high risk should be prioritized
for intervention before significant revenue deterioration occurs.

Account managers should document the reason for intervention,
the evidence supporting the decision, and the recommended
follow-up action.
